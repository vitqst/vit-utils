# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in `vit.tools` to be welcoming, safe, and constructive
for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Welcome different perspectives and levels of experience.
- Give and receive technical feedback without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Focus discussion on improving the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, trolling, sustained
disruption, personal attacks, and publication of private information are not
accepted in project spaces.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, and other
spaces where someone represents the project. The maintainer may edit or remove
content, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary.

For urgent or sensitive conduct reports, contact the maintainer privately
through the contact options on the
[`vitqst` GitHub profile](https://github.com/vitqst). Do not open a public issue
containing personal or sensitive information. Reports will be reviewed as
promptly and impartially as possible, with confidentiality respected where
practical.

This policy is informed by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/).
